Creative workflows in motion.

Fashion editorial

Three individual looks. One group portrait.

  1. 01

    Pair the references

    Model portrait + outfit photo→Matched reference pair

    Pair each portrait with the outfit that person should wear.

  2. 02

    Build each look

    Three matched pairs→Three full-length looks

    Generate each look separately so the face, hijab and garment stay tied to the right person.

  3. 03

    Compose the group

    Three full-length looks→Group editorial image

    Bring the three people into one composition, keeping each person in their own outfit.

  4. 04

    Direct a scene variation

    Three looks + scene reference→Second editorial image

    Use a separate reference for setting, lighting and poses. The same three looks carry into the new scene.

Product banners

One product set. Two creative routes.

  1. 01

    Gather product photos

    Individual product photos→Product set

    Choose the products that need to appear together. Both banner routes start with this set.

  2. 02

    Route A · Compose directly

    Product set→Direct banner

    Arrange the products into one banner using the product photos themselves. This route finishes here.

  3. 03

    Route B · Guide the layout

    Product set + inspiration image→Reference-led banner

    Start again from the same products. Add a visual reference to guide their arrangement and the overall layout.

  4. 04

    Adapt route B

    Reference-led banner→Square + vertical versions

    Reframe the reference-led composition for a square placement and a tall vertical placement.

Product demo video

Two references become an eight-second demo.

  1. 01

    Stage the product

    Product photo + model portrait→Scene brief

    Describe who presents the product, how they hold it and where the demonstration takes place.

  2. 02

    Create the opening frame

    References + scene brief→Opening still

    Generate the presenter holding the product in the chosen setting. This still anchors the video.

  3. 03

    Plan shots and speech

    Opening still→Shot list + voiceover script

    Break the eight seconds into shots and pair the action with what the presenter will say.

  4. 04

    Generate the demo

    Opening still + timed script→Eight-second video

    Use the opening image and timed directions to generate the product demonstration.

  5. 05

    Extract a still

    Finished video→Selected video frame

    Select a moment from the finished clip and extract it as a separate image.
